Who We Are

Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NCEP) is a community-driven non-profit organisation dedicated to empowering young people and vulnerable communities through skills development, creative arts, personal growth, and social development programmes.

Our Vision

To build empowered communities where young people have the skills, confidence, and opportunities to create successful futures.

Our Mission

To provide transformative programmes that develop talent, inspire creativity, and empower communities through education, mentorship, and social development.

Our Core Pillars

NCEP centers its efforts around creative discipline and expressive arts as pathways for personal and professional growth.

Acting

Training in stage performance, character development, and screen execution.

Music

Nurturing vocal talents, rhythm, and stage presence for musical artists.

Writing

Empowering storytellers through creative writing, scriptwriting, and literature.

Poetry & Art

Fostering emotional expression, spoken word, and visual visual creativity.

Who Is Nomduva Community Empowerment Project?

Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NCEP) is a non-profit community organisation focused on empowering young people and disadvantaged communities through skills development, creative arts, education, mentorship, and social development programmes.

Our Vision

To create empowered communities where young people have opportunities to develop their talents, skills, and potential to build successful futures.

Our Mission

To provide programmes that encourage personal growth, creativity, leadership, and economic opportunities for youth and community members.

Our Core Pillars

We utilise arts, culture, and skills development as key tools for positive social change and youth empowerment.

Acting

Stage performance, screen presence, character development, and theatrical execution.

Music

Vocal technique, songwriting, rhythm, and live stage performance mastery.

Writing

Creative writing, scriptwriting, storytelling, and literary composition.

Poetry & Art

Spoken word, public expression, emotional articulation, and visual arts.

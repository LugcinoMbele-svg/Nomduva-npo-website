Who We Are

Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NCEP) is a registered non-profit organization (NPO Registration: 308-012) established to uplift disadvantaged communities through practical skills, creative development, and social support.

Our Vision

To build empowered, self-reliant, and prosperous communities where every individual realizes their full potential.

Our Mission

To transform lives through innovative programs promoting education, youth empowerment, talent discovery, and economic inclusion.

Core Values

Integrity, Community First, Empowerment, Excellence, Innovation, and Collaboration.

Key Areas of Impact

Youth & Talent Development

Mentorship, creative arts, and platforms like Ikhaya Golden Mic to combat drug abuse and unemployment.

Skills & Training

Computer literacy, sewing, baking, and practical skills for sustainable livelihoods.

Women Empowerment

Building self-esteem, financial literacy, and support networks for women across communities.

Early Childhood & Social Care

Feeding schemes, ECD support, and care initiatives for persons living with disabilities.

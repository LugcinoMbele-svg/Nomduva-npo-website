Who We Are

Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NCEP) is a registered Non-Profit Organisation committed to improving lives through community development initiatives. Guided by our constitution, we focus on youth empowerment, women empowerment, education, creative arts, skills development, disability inclusion, anti-drug awareness, entrepreneurship and social development.

Our Story

Nomduva was founded with one vision: to create opportunities where hope is limited. We believe every young person deserves access to education, mentorship, leadership and opportunities that unlock their potential.

Through strategic partnerships and innovative programmes, we continue building stronger communities across KwaZulu-Natal and beyond.

Our Vision

To build empowered, self-reliant and resilient communities where every individual has equal opportunities to succeed and contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To empower communities through education, skills development, creative arts, entrepreneurship, leadership development and sustainable community programmes.

Flagship Programme

Ikhaya Golden Mic

Ikhaya Golden Mic is Nomduva's flagship youth talent development programme created to discover, mentor and showcase talented young South Africans in singing, acting, poetry, dance, comedy, public speaking and cultural performance.

OUR IMPACT

Previous Projects & Achievements

Every programme we deliver is designed to create lasting change. Through education, leadership and creative arts, we continue to empower young people and strengthen communities.

16 June 2026

Youth Day Acting & Creative Writing Workshop

Hosted at Mpumalanga Unit 6 Hall, Hammarsdale, this successful Youth Day programme brought together young people to participate in acting, public speaking, creative writing and confidence-building activities.

Facilitated by Miss Busisiwe Ntuli and Miss Slindile Mhlungu, the workshop created a safe environment where young people expressed themselves, developed leadership skills and gained confidence through the creative arts.

Certificates Awarded

Every participant successfully completed the programme and received a Certificate of Participation recognising their commitment and personal growth.

Launch of Ikhaya Golden Mic

During the workshop, Nomduva officially introduced the Ikhaya Golden Mic programme, receiving enthusiastic support from participants, parents and community members.

Community Participation

Parents, guardians and local residents actively participated in celebrating young people's achievements, strengthening community support for youth development.

Creative Arts Development

Participants improved their confidence, communication, acting, storytelling and public speaking skills through practical activities and teamwork.

Building Partnerships

The event strengthened relationships with community leaders, educators, volunteers and supporters committed to creating more opportunities for young people.

Foundation for Future Growth

The success of this programme laid the foundation for the expansion of Ikhaya Golden Mic into a provincial talent development initiative.

About Nomduva NCEP

Building self-reliant, resilient communities through dedicated intervention.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to uplifting underprivileged and township communities through educational intervention, practical skills training, economic inclusion, and creative development. Our primary goal is establishing sustainable systems that break systemic barriers for local youth.

Our Vision

To cultivate self-sufficient, empowered communities where youth access equitable opportunities to unlock their full potential and contribute meaningfully to socio-economic growth.

Career Readiness

Youth provided with job placement coaching and professional development.

50+ Active

Participants currently enrolled across our developmental cohorts.

6 Core Pillars

Focusing on Creative Arts, Entrepreneurship, Youth Employment, Skills Development, Health, and Environmental Care.

Our Community Impact

Real initiatives delivering measurable social change.

Educational Transformation

Providing essential resources, career guidance workshops, and academic interventions that keep youth motivated and in school.

iKhaya Golden Mic Initiative

Equipping young creatives with professional vocal training, theatrical production insights, networking opportunities, and industry exposure.

Community Empowerment Hubs

Collaborating with local stakeholders to establish safe hubs for talent showcases, leadership development, and business workshops.

Executive Leadership & Team

Dedicated leadership driving transparent governance and social transformation.

Busisiwe Ntuli

Founder & Executive Director

Busisiwe is the visionary founder behind Nomduva Community Empowerment Project. She established NCEP to deliver tangible opportunities for youth and cultivate sustainable development within disadvantaged communities.

Under her strategic guidance—including flagship initiatives like iKhaya Golden Mic—the organization nurtures emerging talents, instills leadership skills, and encourages healthy, empowered life choices.

busisiwe.ntuli@nomduva.org.za

Lugcino Ntsuzi

Public Officer

Lugcino manages organizational governance, administration, and public accountability at NCEP. He brings extensive expertise in strategic planning, institutional compliance, and community outreach.

He drives partner development, public communication, and fundraising initiatives designed to expand NCEP’s reach across KwaZulu-Natal. His guiding ethos: "Building opportunities today for stronger communities tomorrow."

Who is Nomduva Community Empowerment Project?

Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NCEP) is a community-driven non-profit organisation dedicated to empowering young people and vulnerable communities through skills development, creative arts, personal growth, and social development programmes.

We exist to create opportunities where individuals can discover their talents, develop confidence, gain practical skills, and participate positively in building their communities.

Our Belief: Every person has potential. With the right support, mentorship, and opportunities, communities can overcome social and economic challenges.

What NCEP Does

Creates youth development opportunities through education and mentorship
Uses arts, culture, and talent as tools for social change
Encourages self-confidence, leadership, and personal growth
Provides platforms for young people to showcase abilities
Promotes community participation and positive lifestyles
Supports young creatives in acting, writing, and performance

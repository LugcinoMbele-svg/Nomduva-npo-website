Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NCEP) is dedicated to youth development, skills training, and social upliftment.

Who We Are

NCEP focuses on creating sustainable development opportunities for young people and families across KwaZulu-Natal. Through hands-on programs, mentorship, and creative initiatives, we aim to bridge the gap to self-sufficiency.

Our Initiatives

Youth Skills Development

Providing essential practical training to equip youth with modern job-ready skillsets.

Community Outreach

Direct support programs addressing immediate social needs across local communities.

Creative Arts

Empowering local talent through creative projects, culture, and expressive development.

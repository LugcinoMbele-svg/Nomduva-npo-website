Our Key Programmes

Empowering vulnerable youth through structured arts development, life skills training, creative mentorship, and active social outreach.

Core Development Focus Areas

Each of our flagship programmes is structured to address specific youth development, talent nurturing, and community upliftment needs.

Flagship Talent Initiative

1. Ikhaya Golden Mic

An intensive talent discovery and performance platform designed to give young creatives exposure, professional training, and mentorship across acting, music, and spoken word.

  • Acting & Theatre: Screen presence, character preparation, and stagecraft.
  • Music & Vocals: Vocal control, songwriting, studio recording discipline, and stage management.
  • Public Speaking & Poetry: Expressive storytelling and spoken word execution.
Explore Ikhaya Golden Mic
Leadership & Development

2. Youth Development Workshops

Interactive capacity-building workshops equipping young leaders with essential personal development, career navigation, and decision-making skills.

  • Confidence building & self-worth reinforcement
  • Leadership skills, ethics, and team dynamics
  • Substance abuse prevention & personal safety awareness
  • Career pathway guidance & interview preparation
Creative Skills

3. Creative Arts & Media Training

Dedicated modules aimed at equipping creative minds with practical technical knowledge to commercialise their artistic talents.

  • Scriptwriting, storyboarding, and dramatic literature
  • Basic video production, camera work, and sound staging
  • Artist branding, self-promotion, and digital portfolio creation
Community Outreach

4. Community Outreach & Social Engagement

Targeted campaigns and local events designed to unite community members, address socio-economic challenges, and advocate for youth safety.

  • Community performance showcases and talent drives
  • Youth dialogues and open community forums
  • Educational & social awareness campaigns in local schools

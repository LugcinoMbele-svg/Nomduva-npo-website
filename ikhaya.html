Bridging the Creative Gap

Unlocking KwaZulu-Natal’s Next Generation of Artists

The local creative industry is overflowing with talent, but access to professional equipment, studio time, and industry guidance remains out of reach for many. iKhaya Golden Mic 2026 offers structured mentorship, technical masterclasses, and stage opportunities to launch emerging voices across KwaZulu-Natal.

What We Offer

Comprehensive creative and business development

Vocal & Production

Studio fundamentals, vocal discipline, song arrangement, and track recording skills.

Stage & Acting

Performance techniques, script study, audition strategy, and stage presence.

Writing & Poetry

Lyricism, narrative design, scriptwriting, and spoken word refinement.

Industry & Royalties

Guidance on copyrighting, royalty registration (SAMRO/CAPASSO), and self-marketing.

Program Timeline

From community talent search to the live finale

Phase Focus Details
Phase 1 Auditions & Selection Open call talent search in Hammarsdale, Mpumalanga Township, and surrounding KZN regions.
Phase 2 Mentorship Bootcamp 6-week hands-on training with industry professionals covering performance and music business.
Phase 3 Studio & Rehearsals Recording original works, mastering vocal sets, and building artist press kits.
Phase 4 Grand Finale Showcase Live production performance in front of talent scouts, community leaders, and industry judges.

Who Can Apply?

We invite passionate young creators to step forward and refine their craft.

  • Age Group: Youth aged 15–35 residing in KwaZulu-Natal
  • Disciplines: Musicians, Vocalists, Actors, Poets, and Scriptwriters
  • Requirement: Dedication to attending workshops and building a career in arts

Ikhaya Golden Mic 2026

"Discovering Talent. Building Futures. Inspiring Communities."

About the Initiative

Ikhaya Golden Mic 2026 is a flagship youth talent development and anti-drug awareness program presented by the Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NPO)[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). The name "Ikhaya" (meaning "Home") reflects our belief that every young person deserves a safe, supportive space to realize their potential[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

This initiative directly addresses youth unemployment, substance abuse, and the lack of creative platforms by discovering, mentoring, and celebrating emerging South African talent[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

  • Multidisciplinary Platform: Music, Poetry, Acting, Dance, Comedy, and Cultural Performances[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
  • Holistic Development: Includes workshops on financial literacy, branding, mental health, and drug awareness[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
  • Industry Connections: Direct mentorship from celebrity judges, music producers, and media figures[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).

Program Journey & Phases

  1. Phase 1: Outreach & Free Registration – Open to all youth in local communities and schools[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
  2. Phase 2: Regional Auditions – Performance evaluations by professional selection panels[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
  3. Phase 3: Development Workshops – Intensive coaching, stagecraft, and life-skills education[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
  4. Phase 4: Semi-Finals – Live stage competition judged by industry celebrities[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
  5. Phase 5: Grand Finale – High-impact public showcase for winners, sponsors, and media[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).

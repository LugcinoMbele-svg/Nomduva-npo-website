About iKhaya Golden Mic

Empowering the next generation of creative artists and performers.

Our Vision for Local Creatives

iKhaya Golden Mic is a flagship creative arts initiative by the Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NCEP). It was established to provide a structured, safe, and professional platform for youth in local townships to express their artistic talents.

We bridge the gap between passion and professional industry readiness by providing vocal training, stage presentation coaching, music production insights, and mentorship from experienced industry personnel.

Program Components

What artists experience during the program.

Vocal & Stage Mastery

Hands-on training focusing on breath control, vocal projection, stage presence, and live performance confidence.

Music & Media Production

Introduction to sound recording, studio etiquette, songwriting workshops, and music distribution basics.

Mentorship & Career Guidance

Direct guidance on artist branding, copyright awareness, financial literacy, and navigating the creative industry responsibly.

Talent Showcases & Events

Live community showcases and competition events where participants perform before industry scouts and public audiences.

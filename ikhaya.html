About Ikhaya Golden Mic

Ikhaya Golden Mic is a flagship youth empowerment project under the Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NPO 308-012)[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). We provide a platform for aspiring young artists to showcase their musical talent, receive professional mentorship, and steer away from substance abuse and social challenges.

1. Auditions & Discovery

Open talent search across local communities to identify passionate, upcoming performing artists and vocalists.

2. Mentorship & Workshops

Finalists undergo workshops on stage performance, music production, studio etiquette, and financial literacy.

3. Showcase Finale

A grand stage event bringing together community, industry leaders, and sponsors to celebrate young talent.

Performance Categories

Vocal Performance

Solo vocalists across Gospel, Afro-pop, R&B, and local contemporary genres.

Rap & Hip-Hop

Lyrical expression highlighting community stories, conscious messaging, and youth experiences.

Poetry & Spoken Word

Powerful spoken word artists sharing stories of resilience, hope, and social awareness.

Dance & Groups

Choreographed dance groups and vocal ensembles demonstrating team coordination and creativity.

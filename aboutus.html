About Nomduva NCEP

Empowering young minds, building resilient communities, and creating sustainable creative pathways in Hammarsdale and beyond.

Who We Are

Dedicated to Community Upliftment & Youth Empowerment

Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NCEP) is a registered Non-Profit Organisation (308-012 NPO) operating in Hammarsdale, KwaZulu-Natal.

We believe that every young person possesses unique talents that, when nurtured, can transform lives and build stronger communities. Through arts, skills development, leadership initiatives, and social support, NCEP provides structured environments for youth to thrive.

NPO Registration: 308-012 NPO

Location: E169 Woodyglan, Hammarsdale, Mpumalanga Township, 3699

Our Mission

To uplift, educate, and empower young people in disadvantaged communities by providing access to performing arts training, creative skill-building, academic guidance, and social support programs.

Our Vision

A thriving, self-sustainable community where youth are equipped with confidence, practical skills, and opportunities to break cycles of poverty and achieve personal and professional success.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every project and workshop we run.

Empowerment

Giving young people the tools, voice, and confidence to lead positive change in their own lives and surroundings.

Integrity & Transparency

Operating with honesty and clear accountability across all community programs and financial contributions.

Inclusivity

Creating safe, welcoming spaces where every youth—regardless of background—can learn, express, and grow.

Nomduva NCEP

Dedicated to youth development, talent empowerment, education, and social upliftment in vulnerable communities.

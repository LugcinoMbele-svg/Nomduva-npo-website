Leadership & Team

Meet the passionate individuals working behind the scenes to drive youth empowerment, creative excellence, and community transformation.

Executive Leadership

Guided by experience, local vision, and a shared dedication to youth development in South Africa.

Busisiwe Ntuli

Founder – Nomduva Community Empowerment Project

Busisiwe Ntuli is a vision-led community leader who established Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NCEP) to address youth vulnerability, unemployment, and lack of creative infrastructure in local townships. Her dedicated leadership guides the strategic direction, partner engagements, and community outreach operations of the organisation.

Lugcino Ntsuzi

Project Coordinator / Public Officer

Lugcino Ntsuzi serves as the Project Coordinator and Public Officer for NCEP. Combining creative writing talent with project implementation, Lugcino manages programme logistics, community engagement, digital communications, and documentation for initiatives like Ikhaya Golden Mic.

Our Governance Principles

Ensuring accountable management, transparency, and sustainable impact across all community programmes.

Accountability

Maintaining full compliance with NPO regulations (308-012 NPO) and delivering regular impact reporting to partners and stakeholders.

Community Engagement

Working directly alongside local youth, schools, families, and community elders to tailor initiatives to ground-level needs.

Mentorship First

Pairing emerging creative artists with experienced industry mentors to foster long-term career growth and personal resilience.

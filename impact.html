Programme Reach & Participant Data

An overview of how our creative and leadership modules are transforming lives and building concrete skills among local youth.

Acting & Theatre Arts

167 Participants

Our largest creative module provides hands-on acting instruction, character analysis, stage presence, and script execution, helping young people gain self-expression, confidence, and public speaking skills.

Music, Vocals & Rap

33 Performing Artists

Nurturing vocalists, lyricists, and instrumentalists through structured vocal coaching, rhythm exercises, songwriting guidance, and live stage performance showcases like Ikhaya Golden Mic.

Skills Accreditation & Certificates

50+ Certificates Issued

Participants who demonstrate dedication and fulfill training requirements receive formal certificates of achievement, boosting their confidence, personal portfolios, and career prospects.

Youth Safety & Mentorship

Community-Wide Impact

By providing safe spaces after school and during weekends, NCEP actively diverts young individuals away from crime, substance abuse, and dangerous idle behaviors into constructive personal growth.

The Difference We Make

Beyond numbers, our initiatives deliver sustainable social and emotional benefits across local families.

Confidence & Self-Worth

Crime & Addiction Prevention

Creating positive community anchors that protect vulnerable youth from negative social pressures and substance abuse.

Career Pathways

Report Overview & Highlights

The 2025 Ikhaya Golden Mic edition brought together young vocalists, poets, and performers from across Hammarsdale and Mpumalanga Township for three weeks of intensive workshops and live showcase rounds.

Key Program Stats:

  • Registered Youth Participants: 120+ artists
  • Workshop Facilitators: 4 industry mentors
  • Audience Attendance: 350+ community members
  • Finalists Awarded Studio Sessions: Top 5 performers

Outcome Summary: Participants completed sessions on stage presence, vocal discipline, and creative writing. All finalists received certificates of achievement and ongoing access to rehearsal space.

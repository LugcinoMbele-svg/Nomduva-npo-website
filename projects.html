Featured Report

Ikhaya Golden Mic Talent Showcase & Graduation

A comprehensive review of our flagship youth talent competition and certificate graduation ceremony held in Hammarsdale.

Report Overview & Highlights

The Ikhaya Golden Mic & Performing Arts initiative brought together young vocalists, actors, and poets from across Hammarsdale and Mpumalanga Township for intensive training and live showcase certificate presentations.

Key Program Stats:

  • Registered Youth Participants: 120+ artists
  • Workshop Facilitators: 4 industry mentors
  • Audience Attendance: 350+ community members
  • Graduating Certificate Recipients: Top youth performers

Outcome Summary: Participants completed modules on screen presence, dramatic acting, vocal control, and creative expression, culminating in an official certificate award ceremony.

Ikhaya Golden Mic Talent Showcase

Hammarsdale / Mpumalanga Township

A major community performance event bringing together local youth across vocal music, rap, acting, and poetry. The showcase provided aspiring creatives with stage experience, professional sound setups, and feedback from industry mentors.

Acting & Theatre Intensive Workshops

Community Arts Development

Focused drama sessions training over 160 young aspiring actors in stage movement, script reading, character portrayal, and screen presence, helping participants build self-confidence and artistic discipline.

Youth Spoken Word & Creative Writing Sessions

Literary Development Programme

Interactive creative writing and poetry circles giving young writers a safe space to express their personal stories, tackle social issues like substance abuse, and refine their storytelling skills.

Community Youth Empowerment & Certificate Ceremonies

Skills & Recognition Drive

Celebratory community gatherings recognizing participants who successfully completed skill modules and performance workshops. Over 50 certificates of completion have been issued to boost participant resumes and morale.
